## Pooled daily backing

The November 4 capacity change replaces per-ticket full-prize reservation for new daily purchases with draw-and-combination backing. Preserve base `get_rules` revision 6 and verify separate `get_liability_rules` revision 1: daily pooling enabled, quick pooling disabled, 420 ordered pairs, 3398-byte canonical `draw-risk`, `v1`, timestamp PDA. New daily purchases must use `buy_pooled_ticket` and v3 snapshots; `buy_ticket` now rejects new daily v2 entries. Quick v2 entries and every previously accepted v2 claim/refund remain unchanged. Never route a v3 ticket through the old full-prize-release handlers.

Before finalization, pooled backing is `N * entry + max_pair_count * (prize - entry)`, including refund obligations that may precede late oracle fulfillment. After a verified finalized result, reconciliation keeps `remaining_winning_count * prize` and releases only the difference in this draw's liability. Reconciliation is idempotent, pays nothing, and never marks losing tickets settled. Winning claims auto-reconcile if needed and remain immutable-owner directed, once-only, and without expiry. Preserve all other draws' and old tickets' global reserves.

Daily checkout must quote the selected ordered pair and draw using actual risk counts and a fresh vault read. Do not gate pooled daily entries with `free / full_prize`; a new combination may need no additional treasury backing. Quotes must match the current wallet/client/configuration, draw and ordered pair; missing or stale metadata/quotes pause new daily purchases without blocking old claims. Quote the first shared risk account's actual rent separately to its creator, never to the prize vault. Quick draws and repeated same-pair bets retain their full possible liabilities; do not promise unlimited fixed prizes from 20 SOL.

Ongoing bulk reveal actions deduplicate by source program and draw timestamp. Review actual oracle fees once per unique draw, require explicit wallet approvals, retain confirmed progress on failure, and never resend automatically. No background keeper or automatic signing is added.
